WHAT WE DO

Turn sensitive data into insight, and keep it protected wherever AI touches it.

Data De-identification

Remove PHI and confidential client details from structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data so it can feed reporting, analytics, and AI.

  • Validation audits of your current process
  • Pipelines built on Presidio and Philter
  • Deploy in your environment or ours (Azure)
  • Ongoing managed service

AI Gateway Implementation

Put a single, governed front door in front of every model your teams use, with PII guardrails, access control, logging, and cost tracking. Vendor-neutral, on the platform that fits you.

  • LiteLLM proxy deployment and configuration
  • Kong AI Gateway setup and policy design
  • Portkey / Prisma AIRS AI Gateway implementation
  • Prompt redaction before data leaves your network
  • Grafana monitoring for usage, cost, and audit trails

AI Implementation

Roll out AI assistants and agents to real teams, with use cases, safeguards, test plans, and training built around how your people actually work.

  • Use case discovery and pilot design
  • Safeguards for document systems and practice tools
  • Self-hosted agent memory and knowledge bases (OpenViking)
  • Governance aligned to the NIST AI RMF
  • Staff training and adoption support

Data Analytics and Reporting

Turn operational data into reporting leaders trust, from modern data platforms and BI dashboards to privacy-safe HTML reports built on de-identified snapshots, with no live connection to your source systems.

  • Azure data platform design and build
  • Power BI and Grafana dashboards and data models
  • Privacy-safe HTML reporting (Observable Framework, Evidence, Quarto)
  • Data quality, cleansing, and validation
  • Process improvement analysis

Protection at rest and in flight

De-identification secures the data you store and analyze. The AI gateway secures what your people send to models. Together they close the gap most organizations leave open.

AT REST EHR / case files → De-identification pipeline → Dashboards and HTML reports
IN FLIGHT Staff and apps → AI gateway with PII guardrails → Claude, OpenAI, Azure, others
